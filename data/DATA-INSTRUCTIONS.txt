RDL simulation data instructions

Current routing: simulation only.

No empirical market data are required or supplied. This directory will contain
declared simulation inputs and derived machine-readable CSV files only.
Stochastic raw outputs belong in /raw-outputs. Plot-ready and table-ready
derived data will be stored under /data/derived once implementation begins.

Any future empirical input would require a new scope decision and a separate
data-science provenance, licensing, cleaning, sampling and validation plan.
