# RDL Figure F06 Caption v1.0

Artefact status: reproducible figure candidate.

**Constant-rate Abel impact and post-completion relaxation in operational
time.** Panel (a) shows the dimensionless impact displacement generated by a
constant signed rate on `0<=u<=T_u` for the declared resilience values
`nu_u T_u` in `{0,0.25,1,4}`. Solid curves are the independent analytic Abel
integrals; open markers are the cell-integrated causal convolution and are
visually coincident at the plotted resolution. The vertical dotted line marks
completion. Panel (b) normalizes each post-completion path by its own completion
impact to isolate relaxation: larger resilience suppresses older forcing more
rapidly. The undamped execution branch grows as the square root of operational
time, while positive resilience produces a crossover as `nu_u u` approaches
one. This is verification of the reduced locally linear full-line response
model. It is not a solution of the nonlinear Volterra equation, a finite-lattice
simulation, empirical calibration, or a claim that operational and calendar
time coincide.
