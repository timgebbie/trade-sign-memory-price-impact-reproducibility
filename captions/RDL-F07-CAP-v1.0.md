# RDL Figure F07 Caption v1.0

Artefact status: reproducible figure candidate.

**Completion impact under declared schedule and clock conventions.** Panel (a)
uses the undamped locally linear Abel benchmark over volumes from `10^-2` to
`10^2`. At fixed operational rate, varying duration gives slope `1/2` in
completion impact. Fixed participation with `eta=0.1` and background flux
`J=10` gives the same rate and square-root slope. At fixed operational horizon,
the rate must instead grow with volume and completion impact has slope `1`;
this dashed curve is a negative control, not a competing universal law. Panel
(b) holds the operational fixed-rate schedule unchanged and maps it through
`U(t)=alpha_U t`. Activity values `0.5`, `1`, and `2` change calendar completion
time while leaving operational completion impact invariant. Both panels use
logarithmic axes. The reference slopes follow from declared analytic schedule
identities; they are not empirical fits, calibration results, or claims about
damped crossover regimes.
