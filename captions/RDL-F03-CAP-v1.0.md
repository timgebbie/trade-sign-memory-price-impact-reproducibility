# RDL Figure F03 Caption v1.0

Artefact status: reproducible figure candidate.

**Exact event-time LMF sign autocorrelation under a normalized zeta
meta-order-length law.** Panel (a) shows the infinite-support renewal result
for tail exponents `alpha_L` in `{1.2,1.4,1.6,1.8}`. Solid curves are exact
Hurwitz-zeta evaluations; dashed curves are the corresponding large-lag
references. Their slopes are `-gamma_epsilon`, with
`gamma_epsilon=alpha_L-1`, so every displayed case is non-summable. Panel (b)
shows the relative difference between the exact and asymptotic expressions;
the dotted line is the predeclared 0.5% W07 acceptance threshold for event lags
of at least 10. These curves verify the declared renewal working form in event
time. They are not empirical estimates, calendar-time correlations, calibrated
fits or evidence about a particular market. The optional seeded Monte Carlo is
retained only as a finite-sample code diagnostic and is not used to draw the
displayed exact curves. Its promotion check is applied to the declared
`alpha_L=1.5` baseline; the heavy-tail display grid is evaluated exactly.
