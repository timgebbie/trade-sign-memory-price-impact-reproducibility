# RDL Figure F05 Caption v1.1

Artefact status: reproducible figure candidate.

**Finite-width response to one child order in operational time.** The singular
ideal impulse is replaced by a top-hat forcing with fixed area `q_0=1`. Panel
(a) holds the declared width at `tau_0=0.01` and compares the undamped response
with resilience values `nu_u=1` and `nu_u=4`; the dotted line marks the end of
delivery. Panel (b) holds `nu_u=0` and varies the pulse width over
`{0.005,0.01,0.02}`. Narrower delivery produces a higher finite peak and
approaches the ideal impulse response away from the origin, while the total
delivered area remains unchanged. Both response axes are logarithmic; their
power-of-ten tick spacing retains the finite peaks and relaxation tails.
Operational time remains linear to preserve the origin and visual alignment
with F02 and F06. Solid curves are analytic top-hat Abel integrals and open
markers are the cell-integrated numerical response. The curves are finite at
every displayed time and vanish before forcing. This is a declared short-time
regularization of a single child order, not cumulative meta-order impact, a
finite-lattice simulation, or empirical calibration.
