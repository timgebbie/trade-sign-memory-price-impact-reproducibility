**Primary, source-based and flux-based sign conventions.** The primary
child-order sign is the active meta-order sign
(`epsilon_m=sigma_m`). Under signed localized forcing
(`mathcal M_{j,m}=sigma_m q_{j,m}` with `q_{j,m}>=0`) and a nonzero aggregate
forcing in the front window, the source-based sign agrees exactly with the
primary sign. Agreement with the sign of the event-induced front-flux increment
requires the additional local assumptions that the response is monotone,
forcing-dominated and resolved over the same event. Diffusion delay, front
movement, curvature, competing background sources, discretization and event
resolution can therefore break flux-sign preservation. The relevant flux
quantity is the event-induced increment, not the raw front flux. This is a
conditional conceptual map: it reports no agreement frequency, lattice/PDE
result, empirical validation or identification with a price or return sign.
